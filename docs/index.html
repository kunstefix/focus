Focus

Stop staring into the void

Focus is a small Mac app that blocks the sites that keep pulling you in. Take a short break when you need one. When it ends, the sites are blocked again and the Eye loses sight of you.

How a break works

Blocking is the normal state. A break is something you ask for, and it always runs out.

  1. Block your list

    Install once and add the sites you reach for out of habit. They stay blocked, even after a restart.

  2. Ask for a break

    Pick 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes and type your password. That small pause is often enough to change your mind.

  3. Let it end on its own

    When the time is up the sites are blocked again, even if your Mac was asleep. Your browser restarts, so tabs left open on them close too.

See how long you looked

The menu bar shows a countdown during a break, and how much of today went to breaks. The habit is easier to change once you can see it.

  • Blocks each site with and without www, over IPv4 and IPv6.
  • Works system-wide through /etc/hosts, not inside one browser.
  • Only touches its own section of that file and leaves your entries alone.
  • Ending a break early never needs a password.

Install

  1. Download Focus from Gumroad, at a price you choose, or free. Open Focus.dmg and drag Focus into Applications.

  2. Open it once. Focus isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS blocks it the first time. Go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. Or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Focus.app in Terminal.

  3. Click Install in the Focus window and enter your password. Your sites are blocked from then on.
